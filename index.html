Terms & Conditions.

Please read these terms before using Express Your Story.

This is an initial policy draft. It must be reviewed and finalized for the actual service, its operating entity, and applicable law before public launch.

1. Eligibility and accounts

You must meet the minimum age required by applicable law and these service terms. Provide accurate account information, protect your sign-in credentials, and notify the service of suspected misuse. Account access must not be shared in a way that compromises security.

2. Respectful use

Do not threaten, harass, impersonate, stalk, exploit, or unlawfully target others. Do not publish unlawful content, malware, spam, or material that infringes another person's rights.

3. Your content

You retain rights you have in the content you create. You grant the service only the limited permissions necessary to store, process, and display content according to your chosen settings and the service's operation.

4. Privacy and visibility

Audience settings help control who can view your content. No online service can promise that a recipient will not copy or capture material they are permitted to view. Do not share information that you are not comfortable disclosing.

5. Messages and connections

Private messaging is intended for accepted connections. Requests may be declined, connections may be blocked, and abusive behaviour may be reported. Do not use the service to send unsolicited or threatening messages.

6. Stories and deletion

Stories are intended to expire after 24 hours. Technical backups, moderation records, and legally required records may persist for a limited period as described in the final Privacy Policy.

7. Safety, moderation and availability

The service may investigate reports and restrict content or accounts when justified. Availability is not guaranteed, and online interactions carry risks. Contact local emergency services for urgent danger; this service is not an emergency response system.

8. Account termination

You may request account deletion. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate security and dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Changes and contact

Terms may change as the service develops. The final published terms must identify the operator, effective date, and a valid support contact before launch.

YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy.

Understand how information should be collected and handled.

This is a draft, not a final legal policy. Actual data practices, retention periods, processors, and contact details must be verified before this policy is published.

1. Information you provide

The service may process your email address, display name, profile details, posts, feelings, stories, letters, friend requests, messages, reports, and preferences needed to provide its features.

2. Technical information

The service provider may process limited device, diagnostic, security, and usage information where necessary to operate and protect the service. Any analytics or advertising must be disclosed accurately before being enabled.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate accounts, display content to permitted audiences, deliver messages and notifications, enforce safety rules, troubleshoot errors, and meet legal obligations.

4. Visibility and sharing

Content is shared according to its audience settings and the permissions of the people involved. Do not assume that a public post, or content shared with another person, can remain completely confidential after it is viewed.

5. Storage and service providers

The planned architecture may use Firebase for authentication and database services and Cloudinary for selected media uploads. Actual providers, data regions, access permissions, and transfers must be confirmed and described accurately before launch.

6. Security

Security controls should include authenticated access, restrictive database rules, server-side authorization, validation, rate limiting, secure secret management, and appropriate monitoring. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and stories

Stories are intended to expire after 24 hours. Automated backend cleanup and appropriate storage lifecycle rules must be configured and tested. Expiration from the interface alone does not guarantee deletion from storage or backups.

8. Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or request deletion of personal information. Account and privacy controls must be implemented before these rights are advertised as available.

9. Children and sensitive content

Do not collect or process children's information outside the requirements of applicable law. Avoid sharing passwords, financial credentials, precise location, or other highly sensitive details in posts and messages.

10. Policy updates and contact

This policy must be updated when data practices change. Before launch, provide the responsible operator's name, a working privacy contact, the effective date, and any legally required disclosures.
